Explore World Chat

World Chat is a shared room where everyone can read what you post. PIGIN gave you the animal username . That’s the name people see beside your messages.

World Chat is designed to be anonymous. Keep personal details like your real name, school, and address out of public messages.

Which name appears in World Chat?

Set up your private profile

Open Settings to save an optional Private Username and change or remove your private profile photo. Other people can see them in DMs and group chats. Clear the name and tap Save to go back to your animal username.

World Chat only shows your animal username to help protect your identity. For your safety, choose a nickname that leaves out your real name and a photo that keeps your identity private. Either username works at sign-in.

Find your account controls

  • Notifications turns alerts for new messages on or off. Use Mute in a chat to silence that conversation.
  • Dark mode switches the app between light and dark.
  • Log out signs you out on this device.
  • Delete User permanently deletes your account after you confirm and enter your password.

Start a direct message

A DM is a one-on-one conversation. Open DMs, use Start a conversation to search for someone’s Private Username or their animal username if they haven’t set one, then choose their name and send a message.

Create a group chat

Groups let several people chat together. In DMs, choose New group, name it, then search for and add at least two other people. Check Selected members before you tap Create group chat. Once it’s created, use Manage group to see who’s in it.

Try the optimized keyboard

Tap the message box in World Chat or a private conversation to open PIGIN’s mobile keyboard. The rows sit closer together now, so your thumbs can move between them more easily.

Tap outside the message box and keyboard to hide it and see more of the chat.

Use the optimized keyboard in any text box

On mobile, tap any text box to open the optimized keyboard, including group names, Private Username, and search. Tap Done to close the keyboard in a single-line field.
